How to read these results. Each test compares donors (not cells): a cytokine-response score is computed per donor and cell type, patients are compared with healthy donors from the same study, and studies are pooled by meta-analysis. The effect is Hedges' g: g = 1 means the patients' average lies one healthy-donor standard deviation above the healthy average. q is the false-discovery-rate adjusted p (q < 0.1 highlighted). These results are shared by both atlases. For the original cell-level analysis of the clonal haematopoiesis cohort, see the CH: cell level tab.

Studies in the atlases

Cell-level analysis — exploratory. This is the original huCIRA analysis of the Heimlich 2024 blood cohort of clonal haematopoiesis (8 TET2 and 9 DNMT3A carriers vs 7 healthy donors). Here every cell is treated as an independent sample and significance comes from gene-set enrichment (GSEA). Because cells of one person are not independent, this inflates significance: on random splits of healthy donors, the same approach called 26% of tests significant, against 0.4% for the donor-level test. Use this view to see where signals point; the Results: donor level tab is the basis of the paper's statistics.

About this explorer

This site shows two curated single-cell RNA-seq atlases assembled from public studies of clonal haematopoiesis (CH), myelodysplastic neoplasms (MDS), MDS/MPN overlap neoplasms, chronic myelomonocytic leukaemia (CMML), myeloproliferative neoplasms and acute myeloid leukaemia (AML), with healthy donors.

  • BM25 — bone marrow and CD34+ progenitor samples from 25 studies.
  • PBMC4 — peripheral blood mononuclear cells from 4 studies, including a CH cohort with TET2 and DNMT3A carriers.

What you are looking at

  • A subsample of cells. To keep the site fast, each atlas shows a stratified subsample (every curated cell type × study is represented; rare types are kept whole). Counts in the legend refer to the subsample.
  • Level 1 / Level 2 labels are the curated lineages and sub-states used in the paper, assigned from marker genes per cluster and lineage-specific re-embedding.
  • Two kinds of results. Results: donor level compares donors (the paper's analysis). CH: cell level shows the original cell-level huCIRA analysis of the clonal haematopoiesis cohort, for exploration.
  • CellTypist labels (four reference models) and author labels are shown for comparison.
  • Gene expression is shown on a log scale (log1p of counts per 10,000; 0 = not detected), quantised to 256 levels. Only a panel of genes is included: marker genes, all genes of the cytokine signatures, cytokine ligands and receptors, and highly variable genes.
  • Cytokine responses use the Human Cytokine Dictionary (huCIRA) signatures: genes that a given cell type switches on after a given cytokine. For each cell whose type has signatures, the score is the average expression of the signature genes minus that of matched control genes, standardised within the cell type. Grey cells have no signature for that cytokine.
  • Response modules follow the huCIRA paper's groups of highly similar cytokines (Oesinghaus et al. 2025, Supplementary Table 11): Interferon response (IL-12, IFN-α1, IFN-β, IFN-ω, IFN-γ), γc cytokines (IL-2, IL-7, IL-15), type 2 (IL-4, IL-13) and OSM/IL-17C/IGF-1. The groups are fixed across cell types and a module is scored from whichever members have a signature in that cell type; every other cytokine is shown singly. Modules are listed in the order of the paper's broader consensus groups (Supplementary Table 10).

Important caveats

  • Per-cell scores are descriptive. Cells from one person are not independent, so the paper's statistics are computed per donor (Results tab).
  • Studies differ in tissue, cell sorting and chemistry. Comparisons across studies mix disease with batch; compare conditions within a study.
  • Donor-level tests need enough cells: a comparison × cell type is tested (reported with a q value and included in the FDR correction) only if the donor pseudobulks hold a median of ≥ 100 cells in each arm; others are marked n.t. and shown in grey as descriptive effects. The GSE285943 donor labelled ‘cytopenia (MF?)’ by the authors (diagnosis uncertain) is excluded from the donor-level analyses; its cells remain in the maps (condition cytopenia MF uncertain).
  • inferCNV calls (BM25) were not specific in our analysis and are shown for transparency only.

Methods in brief

Studies were found with an LLM-assisted search of GEO and CELLxGENE, reads were processed with STARsolo, ambient RNA removed with CellBender and doublets flagged with Scrublet, then each atlas was integrated with scVI. Cell types were curated from markers; donor-level analyses used edgeR/limma pseudobulks and random-effects meta-analysis.
