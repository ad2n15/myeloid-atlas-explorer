# Data licence

The files in `data/` (cell subsamples, embeddings, labels, gene-expression values,
per-cell scores, donor-level result tables) are derived from public
single-cell studies and are released under the
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)**,
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

You may share and adapt them for any purpose, provided you give appropriate credit:

1. **Cite the original studies.** Each dataset comes from a public GEO or CELLxGENE
   deposit, listed with its accession in the explorer's About tab and in
   `data/results/studies.json`. Their terms continue to apply to the underlying data;
   CELLxGENE Discover datasets are themselves distributed under CC BY 4.0.
2. **Cite this resource and the accompanying manuscript** (Dawoud A, Tapper W,
   Cross N, Rackham O; University of Southampton; citation to be added on publication).

The huCIRA Human Cytokine Dictionary signatures used to compute cytokine-response
scores remain subject to the terms of their original publication.

No warranty is given. The data are provided for research use; they are not intended
for clinical decision-making.
